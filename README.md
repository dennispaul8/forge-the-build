# Remix of Remix of The Forge Hub

Build a polished, modern, responsive media platform prototype for an upcoming digital media hub focused on people, technology, innovation, and the processes behind how things are built.



1. Design Reference & Creative Direction



Use Boardroom.tv as the primary UX/UI benchmark and source of inspiration.



The goal is NOT to copy Boardroom.tv, but to capture the qualities we like about it:



- Clean, modern editorial layout

- Strong visual hierarchy

- Premium media/publication feel

- Seamless combination of written editorial content and video

- Large, high-quality imagery

- Strong typography

- Magazine-style content grids

- Clear content categorization

- Engaging storytelling around people, technology, and culture

- Generous whitespace and clean spacing

- Editorial content that feels equally important as video content



The final design should feel like a premium technology/media publication, rather than a traditional corporate website or basic blog.



---



2. Overall Product Concept



Create a digital media platform where users can discover:



- Founder and engineer stories

- Technology and AI innovations

- Behind-the-scenes development processes

- Long-form written articles and scripts

- YouTube edutainment videos

- Trending stories and highlighted content



The platform should make video and written storytelling feel like two parts of the same editorial experience.



Think of the product as a combination of:



- Modern technology publication

- Video-first media platform

- Editorial magazine

- Founder/engineer storytelling hub



---

14. Visual Design System



Use a premium editorial aesthetic.



Color palette:



- Primarily black / off-white / white

- One restrained accent color

- Avoid excessive gradients

- High contrast

- Subtle borders

- Minimal shadows



Typography:

Use a modern sans-serif typeface for UI and body text.



For major editorial headlines, consider a sophisticated serif or distinctive display font to create a premium magazine feel.



Typography should create strong contrast between:



- Headlines

- Category labels

- Body text

- Metadata

- Navigation



Use large, confident headlines.



---



15. Layout & Responsiveness



The website must be fully responsive.



Desktop:



- Wide editorial grid

- Large hero sections

- Multi-column layouts

- Generous whitespace



Tablet:



- Reduce columns

- Maintain strong hierarchy



Mobile:



- Single-column editorial layout

- Horizontally scrollable trending/video sections where appropriate

- Large readable headlines

- Full-width media

- Sticky/mobile navigation

- Touch-friendly buttons



Do not simply shrink the desktop layout. Reorganize sections appropriately for mobile.



---



16. Interactions



Make the prototype feel interactive.



Implement:



- Navigation links

- Category tabs

- Search interaction

- Newsletter form interaction

- Hover effects on cards

- Video play states

- Horizontal scrolling carousels

- Article detail navigation

- "Read Story" buttons

- "Watch Video" buttons

- Smooth scrolling

- Responsive mobile menu



Use subtle animations rather than excessive motion.



Cards can have subtle image zoom/transform effects on hover.

---



19. UX Goal



The most important goal is to demonstrate how editorial journalism, technology storytelling, and video content can coexist within one cohesive media platform.



A visitor should immediately understand:



Who is building? → People



What are they building? → Technology



How are they building it? → Processes



How can I learn more? → Articles + Videos



The experience should feel:



- Premium

- Editorial

- Modern

- Intelligent

- Technology-focused

- Visually engaging

- Easy to navigate



Avoid making it look like a generic SaaS landing page.



Prioritize content discovery, storytelling, typography, visual hierarchy, and media consumption.



Build the prototype with polished placeholder content and imagery so that it looks presentation-ready as a UX/UI concept for a client review.
